# Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I need internet to use this?
**A:** No! Luxury Jewellers ERP works completely offline. All your data stays on your computer.

### Q: Is my data safe?
**A:** Yes. Everything is stored locally. We recommend regular backups to USB or Google Drive.

### Q: How do I backup my data?
**A:** Settings → Backup & Restore → Backup Now. Save to USB drive or cloud storage.

### Q: Can I use this on multiple computers?
**A:** Each installation is for one computer. Contact us for multi-location licensing.

### Q: Does it calculate GST correctly?
**A:** Yes, it uses official CBIC rates for jewellery. Always verify rates before filing returns.

### Q: I forgot my admin password. What do I do?
**A:** Contact us at contact@your-business.com. We can help reset it.

### Q: Can I add more users?
**A:** Yes. Settings → Users → New User. Assign username and password.

### Q: Does this work on Mac or Linux?
**A:** Currently Windows only (10/11). Contact us for other platforms.

### Q: How often should I backup?
**A:** At least daily. For high-volume shops, backup after each day's invoicing.

### Q: Can I restore from an old backup?
**A:** Yes. Settings → Backup & Restore → Restore. The app will keep a safety copy first.

### Q: What if I lose all data?
**A:** If you have no backups, your data cannot be recovered. Always maintain regular backups!
